Teresa B

Booth #39 Room 4
Specializing in: Depression glass, ceramics, vintage kitchenware, books, jewelry, and home decor

Welcome to Room 4 — Where History Has a Story

My Godfather, Uncle John, was my mentor and the inspiration behind everything you see here. For over 40 years, he poured his heart into the antique business. We both shared a deep belief that antiques are far more than just old objects—they are tangible, living pieces of history.

For six and a half years, I traveled monthly from Seattle, Washington, to keep his room alive with treasures. Today, I am proud to be a full-time Florida resident, carrying on his incredible 40-year legacy right here. With our shared background in customer service, my favorite part of the day is helping you find exactly what you're looking for.

Please take your time exploring "our" room, filled with curated collections of Depression glass, ceramics, vintage kitchenware, books, jewelry, and home decor. Thank you for being a part of Uncle John's legacy!
