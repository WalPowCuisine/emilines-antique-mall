Our Story

For more than two decades, Emiline’s has been a staple in the local community. We built this shop on a love for preserving the past and a passion for connecting people with pieces that tell a story.

Over the last 20 years, we have grown into a vibrant marketplace that antique lovers and bargain hunters trust.

The Emiline's Experience

What makes Emiline's truly special is our incredible community of over 50 independent vendors. Each vendor brings their own unique style, expertise and eye for quality.

Because they are always out hunting for new treasures, our shop floor is constantly refreshed with exciting new merchandise. You can always count on finding a wide, reasonably priced selection of vintage goods, rare collectibles and everyday nostalgia.

No two visits to Emiline's are ever the same. Come see what you can discover today!
